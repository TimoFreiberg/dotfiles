---
name: diff-review
description: Use when the operator wants to see, review, or comment on a jj diff (a change, a stack, a range), or asks for the diff in Lavish.
---

Show jj diffs as a Lavish page generated by `jj-diff-html`; don't hand-write
diff HTML. Follow @skill:lavish for the session rules: never open or focus a
window, and give the operator the URL.

1. In the repo, start `jj-diff-html -r '<revset>' --watch` as a tracked
   background job (or `--from A --to B`; no range means `@`, or `@-` when `@`
   is empty). It prints `Wrote <file>`, keeps the page current as the diff
   changes, and lists it under the current herdr task's Artifacts.
2. `lavish-axi "<file>" --no-open`, then poll it in the background as the
   Lavish skill says.
3. Comments arrive with path, side, line range and the quoted lines. Address
   them in the code; the page refreshes itself.
4. When the review is done, `lavish-axi end "<file>"` and stop the watch job.
